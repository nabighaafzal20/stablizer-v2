About the project

What is a transformer-less voltage stabilizer?

Traditional stabilizers rely on bulky iron-core transformers and relays. Our design uses fast power-electronic switching to regulate voltage directly — lighter, quieter, faster, and smarter.

Clean 220 V output

Keeps a stable regulated output even when the incoming mains drifts well above the target.

Over-voltage alerts

Continuously watches the line and raises an instant alert the moment the supply crosses a safe limit.

Compact & light

No heavy transformer core — four-quadrant bidirectional switches do the regulation instead.

IoT monitoring

Live readings of input, output, frequency, efficiency and load — viewable from anywhere.

Under the hood

How the buck regulation works

The converter samples the mains many times per cycle and switches just enough of it through to hold the output at your chosen target.

Sense

The incoming AC voltage and frequency are measured continuously in real time.

Switch (PWM)

High-frequency four-quadrant switching chops the input to the right effective level.

Filter (LC)

An LC filter smooths the switched waveform back into a clean sine wave.

Regulate

A feedback loop locks the output to the target and reports everything to the dashboard.
